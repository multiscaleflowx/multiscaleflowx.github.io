[Abdullah] and [Thejas]'s work on the liquid utilisation bottleneck in interfacial thermal conductance at metal/water interfaces has been [published in the International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer][1], together with [Samy Merabia] (CNRS, Lyon) and [Rohit]. The paper explains why the widely used vibrational spectra overlap fails to predict thermal conductance across metal/water interfaces: the bottleneck lies in how effectively the interfacial liquid utilises its available vibrational modes. Congratulations to [Abdullah] and [Thejas], who contributed equally to the [paper]!
